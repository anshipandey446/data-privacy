
# Practical 1: Data Privacy Audit of Amazon

 Aim-  To conduct a data privacy audit of the Amazon website and identify how user data is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected.

 Website Selected

 Organization: Amazon

 Website: Amazon

 #### Privacy Policy: Amazon Privacy Notice

 ### Requirements

 - Internet connection
- Web browser
- Amazon account (optional)

Procedure

 - Visit the Amazon website.
- Open its Privacy Notice.
- Identify the types of personal information collected.
- Check how the information is used.
- Examine cookies and tracking technologies.
- Check third-party services and data sharing.
- Examine available privacy and security controls.
- Identify possible privacy risks.
- Suggest measures to improve privacy.

 ### Privacy Risks Identified

 - Large amounts of personal and transaction information may be collected and processed.
- Cookies and similar technologies can be used to understand user activity and preferences.
- Third-party service providers may process some user information.
- Compromised customer accounts could expose personal and order-related information.
- Personalized recommendations and advertising may involve analysis of user activity.
- Retention of information required for legal, security, or business purposes may limit immediate deletion in some circumstances.

 ### Recommendations

 - Users should avoid providing unnecessary personal information.
- Use strong, unique passwords for Amazon accounts.
- Enable available multi-factor authentication/security features.
- Review account, privacy, communication, and advertising preferences regularly.
- Review permissions and settings associated with connected services.
- Avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information in account profiles.
- Read Amazon's Privacy Notice to understand how personal information is handled.
- Users should regularly review their account activity and report suspicious activity.

 ### Result

 The Data Privacy Audit of Amazon was successfully performed. The audit identified its data collection, data usage, cookies and tracking technologies, third-party sharing, security measures, and potential privacy risks.

 ### Conclusion

 Amazon provides a Privacy Notice and various measures for protecting customer information. However, because the platform handles personal, payment, order, browsing, and account information, users should remain careful about the information they provide, account security, cookies, personalized services, and third-party data processing.
